How to build

  • Grab — pinch thumb + index over a part. Pinch a palette item to pull out a new one.
  • Move / turn — move your hand to drag; rotate your hand to rotate the part in 3D.
  • Resize — hold a loose part and pinch with your other hand, then spread or close them.
  • Snap — bring parts close: dots show where they fit, the part is pulled in and locks (green dot).
  • Detach — pinch a bolted-on part and pull firmly away.
  • Delete — drop it on the trash, or hover it and press Delete.
  • Simulate — when the checklist is green, press ▶ Simulate and drive it.

Shortcuts: Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Y redo · Delete remove · ? help · Esc close. Pinch feels off? Open ⚙ and try Calibrate.
